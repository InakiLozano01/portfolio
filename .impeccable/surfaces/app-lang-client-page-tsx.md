# Surface: public site (home, project and blog pages)

Mode: Experience. Audience: recruiters, clients, essay readers, design-minded visitors, in EN and ES. Success: any of contact, project opened, CV downloaded, subscription, essay read. Constraints: all content from the CMS/API, server-seeded so nothing waits on a spinner; IL monogram kept; palette pinned (navy, bordeaux, cream, coral); visual direction pinned by the user: minimal cyberpunk, glow, computer/network/brain/AI.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a living neural map of the work. Projects and the technologies they use are real nodes and synapses; signals travel along them. It refuses the category's dark-mode bento-of-cards and terminal-costume portfolio: no fake terminals, no fake dashboards.

OWN-WORLD: Flat colour fields assigned by role, no gradients or glows: navy #1a2433 is the machine (hero, signal trace, skills, header, footer), cream and paper are reading (statement, about, projects, writing, detail bodies), bordeaux #800020 is the personal voice (contact, the hero brain's disc). Coral is only ever a signal. Hairline strokes, crisp square particles, Geist for display and text, Geist Mono for data.

STORY: Within one viewport the visitor sees who Iñaki is and a live map of what he builds with; scrolling, they follow one signal trace through experience and education, see the stack as clustered constellations, open projects, read essays, and write to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed navy canvas: particles assemble the IL monogram, then dissolve into a breathing, rotating brain on a flat bordeaux disc with heartbeat rings, cascading signals and real project names, while a flow field of particles drifts across the whole hero; name left-aligned in large Geist, each word rising out of its own mask, with a coral full stop after the F, role line beneath in Geist Sans (adapted from Geist Mono: the role line is prose, and the craft floor bans monospace as a costume for "technical"; mono stays for data such as dates, counts and graph labels), primary "Get in touch" (cream fill on navy) and secondary "View work". Cursor near nodes fires them; hovering a node names it. Header is a slim bar with the IL mark.

FORM: Pinned by the user ("cyberpunk ... minimal ... computer/network/brain/ai"), after rolls 1d14dafa, reroll 1 and safer reroll 2 were declined; signature interaction: the synapse graph built from real project-technology data, echoed by the scroll-drawn signal trace.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
